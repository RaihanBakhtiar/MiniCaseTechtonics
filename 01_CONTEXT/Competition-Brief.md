## The case

Terang AI is an AI education technology company offering end-to-end, personalized, adaptive, affordable preparation for exams and interviews. It uses commercial-market revenue to support free access for students in Indonesia's 3T regions. Since October 2024, the casebook reports 10,000+ web users, 5 institutional clients, 20+ organizational collaborators, and 1,500 students supported in 3T zones.

The planned Terang AI App launches on Android and iOS at the end of 2026. The core question is: **how can Terang AI grow from 1,000 to 1,000,000 Play Store users while converting free users into premium subscribers?**

## Required strategic coverage

1. Target segment and relevant positioning.
2. Acquisition system: channel choice, CAC target, campaigns, content, creators, ASO, partnerships and measurement.
3. Activation and retention: onboarding, first value moment, habit/engagement mechanics.
4. Conversion and monetization: funnel, free/premium boundary, offer timing, pricing, trials/promos/referrals.
5. Growth and monetization model: CAC, LTV, activation/retention, paid users and revenue.
6. 6-12 month implementation roadmap, budget, KPIs, risks and mitigations.

## Operating facts and constraints

- B2C model: freemium; full exam practice, interview simulation and personal analysis starts at Rp25,000/month.
- Target market: senior high school students, university students, and young professionals preparing for UTBK, LPDP, CPNS, or job selection.
- Existing 10,000+ website users are a launch asset.
- Constraints: limited people and budget, intense Indonesian edtech competition, price sensitivity, seasonality, and need for trust in AI.
- A strategy must explain its end-to-end system: target, sourcing, content/distribution, conversion/measurement, budget/timing/risks.
