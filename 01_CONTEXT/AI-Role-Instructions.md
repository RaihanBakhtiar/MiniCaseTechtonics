# AI Role Instructions

Use these AI roles as a relay, not as three independent authors. Never treat AI output as evidence without verification.

## ChatGPT - project strategist and integrator

**Use for:** decomposing the case, meeting agendas, synthesis, hypothesis generation, task tracking, draft structures, and turning validated inputs into a coherent deck narrative.

**Prompt starter:**

> You are the project strategist for MCC Techtonic x Terang.AI. Work only from the attached/quoted evidence and stated assumptions. Help us [task]. Return: (1) recommendation, (2) rationale, (3) assumptions, (4) data still needed, (5) a concise table or next actions. Do not invent market data or sources.

## Claude - critical reviewer and logic stress-tester

**Use for:** challenging segment selection, exposing gaps, testing causal logic, reviewing financial assumptions, checking judge-criteria coverage, and editing for clarity.

**Prompt starter:**

> Act as a skeptical case-competition judge. Critique the material below against the Terang.AI case requirements. Identify unsupported claims, faulty causal links, missing feasibility details, risky assumptions, and the three highest-leverage fixes. Preserve good ideas; do not invent evidence.

## Antigravity - analyst and visual/model builder

**Use for:** clean tables, spreadsheet logic, funnel calculations, scenario models, charts, roadmap visuals, and slide-friendly diagrams based on approved inputs.

**Prompt starter:**

> Build an editable [spreadsheet/chart/diagram] using only these validated inputs and explicit assumptions. Include formulas, units, scenario switches, and a source/assumption note. Flag missing inputs instead of fabricating them.

## Shared quality gate

Before a claim reaches the deck, record: source or **ASSUMPTION**, owner, date, formula (where relevant), and the decision it informs.
