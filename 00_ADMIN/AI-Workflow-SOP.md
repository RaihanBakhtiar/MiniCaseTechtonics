# AI Workflow SOP - Exact Handoff by Stage

## Non-negotiables

- Official case PDFs are the authority; external research needs a traceable source.
- AI may propose; the team validates and owns the decision.
- Put raw prompts/outputs only when useful; store final decisions in the matching template.

| Stage | Human owner/action | ChatGPT | Claude | Antigravity | Output / gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Set up | Raihan opens tracker; team reads brief | Turn case into question tree and meeting agenda | Check interpretation of case requirements | Create blank funnel/model tables if needed | Agreed scope, roles, deadlines |
| 1. Research | Tio gathers credible market, customer and competitor sources | Create research plan, interview/survey questions, insight synthesis | Challenge source quality and insight leaps | Clean research tables; size market scenarios | Evidence log and priority-segment shortlist |
| 2. Choose thesis | All decide one segment and problem | Draft 2-3 strategic theses and scorecard | Red-team each thesis; surface objections | Build comparison matrix | One approved thesis with assumptions |
| 3. Design strategy | Habibi designs connected acquisition-to-retention system | Map funnel, campaigns, growth loops and user journey | Test causal chain, operational burden and differentiation | Diagram funnel/journey and calculate targets | GTM blueprint with owner and KPI per stage |
| 4. Monetize | Raihan defines offer/pricing economics | Draft free/premium boundaries and offer moments | Critique willingness-to-pay and cannibalization logic | Build unit economics and scenarios | Approved pricing, funnel targets, CAC/LTV model |
| 5. Implement | Raihan drafts roadmap and budget | Convert strategy into 6-12 month milestones, risks, owners | Find missing dependencies and impossible sequencing | Gantt/timeline, budget and KPI dashboard | Feasible roadmap plus risk plan |
| 6. Build deck | Raihan integrates, all review | Produce 10-page outline and concise slide copy | Judge-style scoring review and claim audit | Create final charts/tables/visual assets | English 16:9 deck draft |
| 7. Final QA | All validate PDF | Checklist against brief and deck flow | Final skeptical review | Verify figures/chart labels and calculations | Submission-ready PDF and archive snapshot |

## Required prompt inputs at each handoff

Give the next AI: objective, audience/segment, evidence, existing decision, constraints, desired format, and explicit assumptions. Ask it to flag missing data rather than invent it.

## Daily cadence

1. Update `Task-Tracker.md` before the check-in.
2. Each owner states: output completed, evidence added, decision requested, blocker.
3. Raihan records decisions in Team Context and reconciles changes across templates.
4. Run Claude review after any major strategy or model change.
