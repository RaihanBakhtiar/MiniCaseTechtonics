## Working agreements

- One shared thesis: one priority segment, one positioning, one connected funnel.
- Use evidence before opinions; label estimates as **ASSUMPTION**.
- Daily 15-minute check-in: blockers, decisions needed, outputs due.
- Raihan maintains the integrated narrative and version list; all three approve final claims.
- Store raw evidence in `02_RESEARCH`; store deck-ready decisions in `03_STRATEGY` or `05_ROADMAP_KPIS`.

## First meeting (60 minutes)

1. Read the competition brief (10 min).
2. Agree the candidate priority segment and its key job-to-be-done (15 min).
3. List the three biggest barriers across acquisition, activation/retention, and conversion (10 min).
4. Draft one testable solution thesis and decide evidence needed (15 min).
5. Assign tracker tasks, owners, and first check-in (10 min).
